## Notes

DRZ3MS's steering crank is only compatible with servos with 3.5 to 3.9mm spline. A few examples from GT55racing.com: 
-Atomic 1885
-Atomic 1820 series
-Atomic BZ-UP017 series
-AGFRC A06CLS series
